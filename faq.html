Category 01

Corporate Leasing & Fleet Sizing

Essential questions regarding corporate accounts, lease structures, and vehicle deployment.

To open a commercial account, we require your corporate certificate of incorporation, federal tax ID (EIN), audited financial statements from the past two fiscal years, and authorized signatory credentials. Our credit department finishes evaluations within 24 to 48 business hours.
Yes. We provide complete factory-grade decal and vinyl wrap upfitting, internal cargo shelving installations, and safety cage modifications. All upfitting can be amortized across your monthly lease schedule to prevent major upfront capital expenditures.
In-stock commercial sedans, SUVs, and standard transit vans are typically delivered to your preferred hub in 5 to 7 business days. Custom heavy-duty chassis or specialty refrigeration vehicles usually require 3 to 5 weeks depending on manufacturer queues.
We use pooled mileage across your entire active fleet rather than assessing each car individually, which safeguards your business against isolated high-mileage runs. Normal industrial wear guidelines are established in transparent condition schedules prior to contract signing.
Yes. Our master service contracts feature flexible scale riders that enable seasonal fleet expansion for peak operations or vehicle exchange options if your payload requirements shift before the full lease term expires.
You may seamlessly rotate into current-model vehicles under an updated lease, extend your prevailing agreement on month-to-month terms, or exercise your pre-determined fair-market-value buyout rider to assume full vehicle equity.
Category 02

Telematics, Maintenance & Support

Details on vehicle telemetry hardware, proactive repairs, and driver assistance programs.

All leased units come equipped with pre-calibrated OBD-II and CAN bus transceivers that sync location, harsh braking events, idling duration, and odometer metrics directly to your dashboard in sub-second latency via encrypted cellular bands.
Standard enterprise subscriptions cover all scheduled manufacturer servicings, oil changes, tire rotations, brake pad replacements, and fluid top-offs through our nationwide network of more than 15,000 certified service facilities.
When an onboard sensor flags a diagnostic trouble code (such as transmission slip or low oil pressure), an automated alert notifies our fleet management center. We prompt your driver with nearest booking windows to eliminate terminal vehicle breakdowns.
Yes. The web dashboard enables you to create polygonal boundaries around work zones, customer sites, and warehouses. Real-time push, SMS, or email alerts trigger when a vehicle departs designated territory or starts up outside pre-approved hours.
FleetManage is SOC 2 Type II compliant and processes all telemetry data using end-to-end TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 at rest. Driver biometric and telemetry logs remain isolated to your company tenant with granular role-based access restrictions.
Drivers have one-touch emergency response via the driver mobile app or our 24/7 dedicated dispatch hotline. We immediately route nearest recovery flatbeds, manage emergency towing, open insurance claims, and dispatch replacement rental units.
