Fleet Leasing & Pricing FAQ

Detailed answers to corporate inquiries on credit underwriting, upfitting amortization, and lease terms.

How does commercial vehicle leasing compare to outright vehicle purchasing?

Commercial vehicle leasing preserves your business credit lines and operating capital. Instead of deploying $50,000 to $150,000 upfront per vehicle in depreciating assets, your company pays a predictable monthly operating expense that includes telematics, warranty coverage, and nationwide maintenance. Operating lease payments are also generally 100% tax-deductible.

Can we amortize custom vinyl vehicle wraps, cargo shelving, and liftgates into the lease?

Yes! Our factory-grade upfitting program covers custom vinyl branding wraps, commercial shelving units, ladder racks, hydraulic liftgates, and refrigeration units. All equipment costs are rolled directly into your regular monthly lease schedule, requiring $0 upfront capital.

What happens if an operational vehicle exceeds its estimated annual mileage?

Unlike consumer car leases, FleetManage implements a Pooled Fleet Mileage System. Mileage across your entire active commercial fleet is aggregated, meaning lower-mileage vehicles balance out intensive routes. Under our Open-End TRAC leases, there are zero mileage penalties whatsoever.

Can we lease vehicles in one state and operate them across multiple regional depots?

Absolutely. FleetManage handles multi-state licensing, title registration, and local tax compliance across all 50 states. We dispatch vehicles directly to your regional fulfillment centers, depots, or field offices.
