Quick Answers

Frequently Inquired Fleet Topics

How fast can leased commercial vehicles be delivered?

Standard inventory cargo vans and pickup trucks can be staged and delivered in as little as 7 to 14 business days. Custom upfitted box trucks (with specialized refrigeration, hydraulic liftgates, or custom racks) typically take 25 to 45 business days.

Can we enroll our existing company-owned vehicles into your Fleet Management?

Yes! We offer a popular Sale & Leaseback program to unlock working capital from your existing assets. Alternatively, you can subscribe to our Telematics & Maintenance Management package to bring existing vehicles into our tracking and 850+ garage servicing network without selling them.

What is the difference between Open-End TRAC and Closed-End leases?

Open-End TRAC (Terminal Rental Adjustment Clause) leases have no mileage penalties; if the vehicle sells for more than its book value at the end of term, the surplus is returned to you. Closed-End leases allow you to simply turn the vehicle in at term completion without residual market risk.

How are maintenance approvals handled on the road?

Drivers pull into any of our 850+ certified national service centers and present their FleetManage Corporate ID. The shop connects directly to our automated electronic billing portal, eliminating driver out-of-pocket expenses and pre-authorizing repairs in under 15 minutes.

Need Immediate Assistance?

If your fleet is experiencing an active disruption or you require urgent technical intervention, bypass the form and call our critical response team directly.
