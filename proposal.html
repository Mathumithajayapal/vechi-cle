How quickly can vehicles be delivered once a proposal is accepted?

Standard production commercial cargo vans and box trucks are dispatched from our regional vehicle staging hubs within 7 to 14 business days. Custom specialized upfits (e.g. multi-temp reefer units or crane bodies) typically require 21 to 30 days.

Can existing company-owned vehicles be transitioned into this agreement?

Yes! Through our **Sale-Leaseback Program**, FleetManage will purchase your existing commercial vehicles for cash at fair market value and immediately lease them back to you under a TRAC lease, unlocking working capital while adding our maintenance and telematics network.

Are upfitting and branding vinyl wrap costs rolled into the monthly lease?

Absolutely. All shelving packages, commercial ladder racks, refrigeration units, power inverters, telematics hardware, and full corporate vinyl graphics are 100% capitalized into your single monthly lease payment.
